Velox

A terminal-native autonomous equity trading system on Alpaca. Six strategies feed an 18-stage signal pipeline with ML confidence gating, a unified exit orchestrator, real-time VaR and a tiered circuit breaker, all driven from one velox command. It aims for consistent returns over big wins, with targets of 0.3–0.8% per trade and a 65–75% win rate, and trades on a paper account by default.

Risk Management

Multi-layered risk controls

18-Stage Signal Pipeline

Every signal is qualified through data quality, multi-timeframe confluence, transaction-cost, VaR, correlation and sector checks, then ML confidence gating.

Exit Orchestrator

One exit system with a 10-level priority cascade replaces three competing ones, so the most urgent reason always wins, from the kill switch down to trailing stops.

Circuit Breaker

Four tiers from -1% to -4% on the day: halve new sizes, stop entries, close day trades, then kill everything. A VIX spike can trip it too.

VaR Monitoring

Parametric (95% and 99%), historical and 10,000-path Monte Carlo Value-at-Risk, checked continuously.

Beta Neutralizer

Every 15 minutes it checks portfolio beta and hedges with SPY when the absolute beta passes 0.3.

Correlation Limiter

Eigenvalue-based effective bets and a 30% sector cap, refreshed intraday, keep correlated positions from piling up.

Intelligence

ML-powered decision making

ML Ensemble

A four-model averaging ensemble tuned with Optuna on 50 core features (cut from 200+), reaching 0.947 AUC.

FinBERT Sentiment

A local NLP model scores earnings and news sentiment with no paid API, feeding the PEAD strategy.

Adaptive Scanning

Scan intervals adapt to market activity, with signal stacking and dynamic stops on winning trades.
